Reporting criteria
Regional totals count unique client names; within a country each client is counted once. Clients declared in several countries are replicated per country (Justo in Chile, Mexico and Colombia; Fintoc in Chile and Peru; Yuno in four countries), so the sum across countries is higher than the unique regional total. Clients with several operation modes are counted once in each declared mode, so the sum across modes can exceed the client count. Operation modes: Standalone, Cloud Sync, Cloud Async, App to App Sync and App to App Async. Coverage is the share of cells that meet all three readiness criteria: at least one client in production with that operation mode or service, at least 200 transactions across those clients in the last week, and no critical issues for that country and mode or service. Green means all three are met, amber means at least one is missing, red means no criterion is met or there are critical issues, and grey “–” means there are no clients at all in integration or production. App to App Async is excluded from the matrix, as both apps run on the same terminal and async calls add little value there. Clicking a cell shows the clients behind it, the clients in pipeline, the weekly transactions and the open issues with the date the last ticket is resolved. Pipeline clients never count towards readiness or coverage, since they have not started integrating. Client counts come from the spreadsheet; weekly transaction volumes and the per-cell issue lists are still placeholder data. Critical issues cover high, urgent and immediate criticality affecting clients in production or integration. Service mapping from the spreadsheet: Charge → Charge; Void → Refunds; Other Integration Services → Other Services. DMS is an issue scope, not a separate group. Dates are shown as DD/MM/YYYY. Release status is derived from the production shipping date; shipping to production does not imply a per-terminal deployment status.
